On Omarchy 4.5 the widget shows up beside the clock again, and it now gives width back there before it runs into the rest of the bar, as it already did at the ends. This release also adds optional 10 second seek buttons and draws the controls at Omarchy's bar icon size.

**Update** with `omarchy plugin update crmne.mpris`, or install with `omarchy plugin add https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris.git --enable --yes`.

## New

- **Skip 10 seconds back or forward.** Turn on "Show seek buttons" in the widget's appearance panel (right-click the widget) for back and forward buttons around play/pause, handy for podcasts, audiobooks and videos. They are off by default, disabled when the player cannot seek, and leave the bar together with previous and next when space runs short. `omarchy-shell crmne.mpris seekBy -10` does the same from a script or a key binding. Requested in [#21](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/issues/21) and built in [#22](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/pull/22) by @freezombie.

  ![The widget beside the clock with the seek buttons on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/v1.2.0/packaging/release-notes/v1.2.0-seek.png)

- **The widget adapts beside the clock too.** In Omarchy 4.5's center sections it now measures the room it has, as it does in the left and right sections: a long title shortens first, then the controls and artwork step aside, before it reaches the next section. Displays with a notch, where the center sections sit beside the right one, are covered as well. By @crmne, building on [#26](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/pull/26).

  ![On a narrow bar the title shortens beside the clock instead of running into the tray](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/v1.2.0/packaging/release-notes/v1.2.0-center.png)

## Fixed

- **The widget shows up again in the middle of the bar on Omarchy 4.5.** Omarchy 4.5 split the middle of the bar into three sections and moved widgets beside the clock into the new `centerEnd`, which the widget did not recognise, so it hid itself there completely. It now knows the new sections, and on a bar it cannot measure it keeps its full layout instead of hiding. Reported by @Switchmans in [#25](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/issues/25). By @freezombie in [#26](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/pull/26), with the fallback by @crmne.
- **The controls are drawn at Omarchy's bar icon size and centered on their shapes.** Previous and next used to paint noticeably smaller than the seek buttons and the artwork's music note, and each icon sat a little off the middle of its button. By @crmne.
- **No more color warnings in the shell's log on Omarchy 4.5.** Qt 6.12 hid Omarchy's color palette from the widget, so the switches in its appearance panel were handed no accent color, with a warning for each one on every bar. By @crmne; thanks @freezombie, who spotted the cause in [#22](https://github.com/crmne/omarchy-mpris/pull/22).

## Known limitations

- With JetBrainsMono Nerd Font, previous, next and play/pause sit up to a pixel above the middle of the bar. The widget now centers its icons the way Omarchy's own bar icons are centered, which measures these glyphs down to the text baseline.
- In the tray's drawer the widget keeps its full layout rather than adapting.
